stake2.one

Short links for single pool operators. stake2.one/TICKER opens the page of that pool on Cardanoscan.

The name says what it is for: Staking to a pool of a single pool operator.

623 pools: Every pool that is the only pool of its owner, with a public name, ticker and website, a relay that answers and its pledge met, below the saturation point at k = 1000, and not of an exchange or a founding entity. The list is renewed every day from the Cardano chain. No sign up and no cost.
